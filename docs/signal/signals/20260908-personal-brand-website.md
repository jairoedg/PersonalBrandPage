## The Signal

Jairo Diaz lacks a dedicated professional portfolio website, and the traditional 2-page CV format cannot convey the depth of his project experience, leadership approach, or business value; this creates an opportunity to build a site that positions him as a trusted technology delivery leader by demonstrating how he transforms business initiatives into successful technology outcomes through people, collaboration, and predictable execution. `[OPINION — rationale: CVs are inherently concise and the foundation documents explicitly state that the current profile does not fully communicate breadth of experience; user confirmed the goal is to use the website as an expanded professional reference]`

- Three approved foundation documents exist and define the strategic context for the engagement: `00-foundation/01-personal-brand-blueprint.md`, `00-foundation/02-website-product-vision.md`, and `00-foundation/03-recruiter-journey.md`. `[FACT — source: files created 2026-09-04, content reviewed]`
- A current CV (`00-foundation/CV JairoDiaz - Project Manager.docx`) has been converted to `explore/sources/CV JairoDiaz - Project Manager.md` and provides a concise professional summary, education, certifications, languages, courses, and work experience. `[FACT — source: converted CV, 2026-09-08]`
- The product vision document states that the portfolio should extend the traditional CV by providing structured project case studies, measurable achievements, leadership philosophy, and evidence of business impact. `[FACT — source: 00-foundation/02-website-product-vision.md]`
- The recruiter journey document frames the website as a decision-support tool: every page should reduce uncertainty and increase confidence until the visitor decides to contact Jairo. `[FACT — source: 00-foundation/03-recruiter-journey.md]`
- The user explicitly stated the objective is to "create a website where I can show not only the projects, but selling myself as someone who can solve issues by leading projects and people." `[FACT — source: user statement during Signal Capture session, 2026-09-08]`

## Why This Matters

- Without a structured digital portfolio, recruiters and hiring managers see only a fraction of Jairo's experience, project complexity remains hidden, business achievements are difficult to demonstrate, differentiation is limited, and opportunities may be lost. `[OPINION — rationale: stated in 00-foundation/02-website-product-vision.md Product Problem Statement]`
- A well-designed website can become the authoritative professional reference for Jairo's career, support consistency across CV, LinkedIn, and Endava profiles, and improve recruiter engagement and interview quality. `[OPINION — rationale: stated in 00-foundation/02-website-product-vision.md Product Objectives and Business Success Metrics]`
- The website is intended to be open to anyone who wants to review professional information in depth, and it will complement — not replace — the short-form CV shared directly with recruiters. `[FACT — source: user clarification during Signal Capture session]`

## Constraints

- The website must remain visually clean, professional, evidence-based, business-oriented, and story-driven per the approved Product Principles. `[FACT — source: 00-foundation/02-website-product-vision.md]`
- The MVP scope is defined as: Homepage, About, Projects, Leadership, Career Journey, How I Can Help, Contact. `[FACT — source: 00-foundation/02-website-product-vision.md MVP section]`
- Out of scope for MVP: AI Chatbot, Blog, Newsletter, interactive architecture diagrams, Delivery Manager positioning, Program Manager positioning, public speaking portfolio. `[FACT — source: 00-foundation/02-website-product-vision.md Out of Scope section]`
- The CV and Endava profile should remain short-form (2-3 pages); the website is the detailed, visual expansion of that profile. `[FACT — source: user clarification during Signal Capture session]`
- Project stories will initially be populated from the current CV and progressively enriched with additional business context, leadership decisions, measurable outcomes, lessons learned, team composition, technologies, and project evidence gathered during the Explore phase. `[ASSUMPTION]`
- The website shall communicate Jairo's professional identity through evidence, storytelling, and project experience rather than acting as a chronological online CV. `[FACT — source: approved Personal Brand Blueprint and Website Strategy]`
