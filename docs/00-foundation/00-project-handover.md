# Strategic Decisions

The following strategic decisions were approved during Discovery.

## The website is NOT

- an online CV
- a project list
- a technology showcase

---

## The website IS

- a professional product
- a decision-support tool
- an evidence-based portfolio
- a storytelling platform
- the authoritative professional reference

---

## Storytelling Principles

Projects are the primary storytelling mechanism.

Evidence always comes before claims.

Leadership is demonstrated through projects.

Business outcomes are more important than technical implementation.

---

## Navigation Principles

The visitor should progressively discover:

Professional Identity

↓

Evidence

↓

Relevant Experience

↓

Leadership

↓

Confidence

↓

Contact

---

## Leadership Positioning

Jairo is positioned as a Delivery Leader capable of connecting business strategy, people, and technology execution.

The website should communicate:

- trust
- predictability
- collaboration
- servant leadership
- business value
