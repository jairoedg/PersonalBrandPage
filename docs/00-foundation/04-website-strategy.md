# Website Strategy

Version: 2.0
Status: Approved
Owner: Jairo Enrique Diaz

---

# Purpose

Define the strategic experience of the Personal Brand Page.

This document describes how visitors should progressively discover Jairo's professional identity, leadership philosophy, project experience, and business value.

It intentionally avoids implementation details and instead focuses on the user experience, information architecture, and storytelling strategy.

---

# Strategic Goal

The website is not intended to be an online résumé.

Its purpose is to become the authoritative professional reference for Jairo Enrique Diaz.

Every page should progressively increase visitor confidence until they reach the conclusion:

> "This is someone I would trust to lead an important technology initiative."

---

# Product Principles

The website should always be:

- Professional
- Business-oriented
- Story-driven
- Evidence-based
- Easy to navigate
- Easy to scan
- Authentic
- Calm
- Human-centered

Avoid:

- Marketing language
- Buzzwords
- Information overload
- Long paragraphs
- Self-promotion without evidence

---

# Navigation Strategy

↓

Related Capability

↓

Leadership Principle

↓

Another Project

↓

Contact

# Storytelling Strategy

Projects are the central storytelling mechanism.

Each project should describe:

Business Context

Business Problem

Project Goals

My Role

Leadership Challenges

Technology Landscape

Delivery Approach

Business Results

Lessons Learned

Evidence

Every project should finish with:

> "What did this experience teach me?"

---

# Evidence Strategy

Claims should never exist without evidence.

Every important statement should be supported by one or more of:

- KPI
- Business Outcome
- Client Recognition
- Team Size
- Geographic Scope
- Delivery Complexity
- Certifications
- Leadership Example
- Lesson Learned

The website should progressively build credibility rather than asking visitors to trust unsupported statements.

---

# Capability Strategy

Visitors should discover capabilities through business problems instead of technical skills.

Examples

Instead of:

Agile

Cloud

Leadership

Prefer:

Helping organizations modernize technology platforms.

Leading distributed engineering teams.

Improving delivery predictability.

Aligning business and technology.

Managing complex stakeholder environments.

Each capability links to supporting projects.

---

# Timeline Strategy

The timeline exists because recruiters expect it.

However, it is not the primary storytelling mechanism.

Its objectives are:

- demonstrate professional evolution
- provide chronological navigation
- reinforce career progression

Detailed learning happens inside Project Stories.

---

Leadership Evolution

# About Strategy

# Contact Strategy

Reduce friction.

Primary actions:

Download CV

LinkedIn

Email

Phone

Everything should require the minimum number of clicks.

# Success Criteria

Within five minutes, a recruiter should confidently answer:

- Who is Jairo?
- What value does he bring?
- Has he solved similar problems?
- How does he lead?
- Can I trust him?
- How do I contact him?

If all six questions are answered, the strategic objective has been achieved.
