# Product Vision

Create a professional digital portfolio that allows recruiters, hiring managers, clients and business stakeholders to quickly understand who Jairo is as a professional, the value he brings to organizations and the experience behind the projects he has led.

The product extends the traditional CV by providing structured project case studies, measurable achievements, leadership philosophy and evidence of business impact.

---

# Why are we building this product?

Traditional CVs are intentionally concise.

While they summarize professional experience, they rarely provide enough context for recruiters and hiring managers to fully understand:

- Project complexity
- Leadership challenges
- Business outcomes
- Delivery approach
- Project scale
- Technologies involved
- Lessons learned
- Measurable value created

This portfolio becomes the authoritative professional reference for Jairo's career.

---

# Product Problem Statement

The current professional profile does not fully communicate the breadth of experience, leadership approach and business value accumulated throughout Jairo's career.

As a consequence:

- recruiters only see a fraction of the experience,
- project complexity is hidden,
- business achievements are difficult to demonstrate,
- differentiation is limited,
- opportunities may be lost.

# Product Objectives

The portfolio should:

- Clearly communicate professional identity.
- Demonstrate leadership philosophy.
- Showcase project experience.
- Provide measurable evidence.
- Build trust through authentic storytelling.
- Differentiate Jairo from other senior Project Managers.
- Support recruiters before interviews.
- Become the reference source for professional information.

---

# Value Proposition

Visitors should understand:

- how Jairo leads,
- how he thinks,
- how he solves problems,
- how he communicates,
- what business value he delivers,
- what it is like to work with him.

---

# Business Success Metrics

Within twelve months the portfolio should contribute to:

- More recruiter contacts.
- More interview invitations.
- Better interview quality.
- Greater visibility for senior delivery opportunities.
- Better consistency between Website, CV and LinkedIn.

---

# User Success Metrics

A recruiter should answer within five minutes:

- Who is Jairo?
- What industries has he worked in?
- What projects has he led?
- Can I trust him?
- Why is he different?
- How do I contact him?
