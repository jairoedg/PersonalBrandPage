# Recruiter Journey

Version: 1.0
Status: Approved

---

# Purpose

Define how recruiters, hiring managers and business leaders navigate the website, the information they seek, and how the website progressively builds confidence until they decide to contact Jairo.

---

# Core Principle

The website is not a digital CV.

It is a decision-support tool.

Every page should reduce uncertainty and increase confidence.

---

# Information Priority

The website should progressively answer:

1. Who is Jairo?
2. Has he actually done this?
3. Does he match our needs?
4. What kind of projects has he led?
5. Why should I trust him?
6. How do I contact him?

---

# Website Success

A visitor should leave the website thinking:

> "I understand not only what Jairo has done, but how he leads, how he works and why he could add value to our organization."
