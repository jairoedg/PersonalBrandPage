## Refined Concepts

These three concepts remain distinct alternatives at different conceptual levels: an individual Project Story; relationships among approved content; and the collective meaning of the approved portfolio evidence. They have not been consolidated, ranked, or selected as a final product direction.

### Concept 1: Credible Story Layers

**ID-5 direction:** Layered Stories & Evidence (including selected ideas 2, 4, 5, 7, 9, 11, 12, 14, 19, and 24).

**Conceptual level:** One individual Project Story.

**Description and contribution:** This concept focuses on how an individual public Project Story establishes orientation to its business context, outcome, and Jairo's contribution, then supports progressively deeper evaluation through its approved narrative and publication-eligible detail. It keeps the public story grounded in a validated Project without exposing raw or restricted source Evidence.

**Value:** P1 can assess experience relevance from concise story context; P2 can examine comparable problems and responses more deeply. The concept supports the overall relevance/confidence objective without imposing a time limit on an individual story.

**Risks and assumptions:** Evidence depth varies by Project Story, and individual Evidence assessment has not been systematically recorded for every claim. Unsupported wording or unclear attribution could undermine credibility. Context AS-03—the sufficiency of existing career material for defensible Project Stories—remains partially validated. A story must not imply that every Project has equal evidence depth.

**Boundaries and open decisions:** Focus is the content and evidence of one story, not cross-story navigation or portfolio-wide capability synthesis. Exact Project Story summary fields, preview selection/ordering, approved wording, public evidence/reference details, and the progressive-disclosure interaction mechanism remain open for PRD / Experience Design. No per-story timer, detailed UI mechanism, or final content is selected.

### Concept 2: Evidence Paths, Not Funnel

**ID-5 direction:** Non-linear Routes (including selected ideas 3, 6, 10, 13, 16, 17, 20, and 22).

**Conceptual level:** Relationships and visitor-selected exploration across approved content.

**Description and contribution:** This concept focuses on how approved relationships among Project Stories, Capabilities, Leadership Principles, and supporting context can enable meaningful visitor-selected exploration. Visitors retain the ability to enter, skip, revisit, or move directly to Contact rather than follow a required sequence. It builds on the approved A10 flows and canonical A3 relationships without specifying an exact set of links.

**Value:** Supports P1 relevance assessment, P2 deeper exploration, and legitimate shorter or exploratory routes across the approved screens. It makes the approved non-linear Recruiter Journey expressible without replacing it with a mandatory funnel.

**Risks and assumptions:** Too many, unclear, or weakly supported links could overwhelm visitors or imply relationships not approved in A3. Context AS-07—the effective translation of the Recruiter Journey into the actual experience—remains an active assumption. P3-specific evidence priorities remain unknown and do not justify dedicated paths.

**Boundaries and open decisions:** Use only canonically supported relationships and approved screens. Exact Related / Next Exploration links and cross-linking patterns remain open. No additional screen, P3-specific navigation, runtime recommendation/personalization, or mandatory sequence is proposed.

### Concept 3: Capability and Growth Threads

**ID-5 direction:** Capability & Evolution (including selected ideas 18, 21, and 23).

**Conceptual level:** The collective meaning of the approved portfolio evidence, capabilities, and professional evolution.

**Description and contribution:** This concept focuses on what the body of approved evidence collectively demonstrates about Jairo's canonical business-problem-focused Capabilities and professional evolution. It connects the approved Capability-to-Project relationships with supporting Project Stories and preserves the Tigo → Avianca → DFA arc as a narrative of changed leadership behavior and applied learning.

**Value:** Helps visitors relate individual evidence to broader capability and professional-growth themes while keeping the Project Stories as the primary public storytelling mechanism. The ordered evolution arc provides context across Career Journey and Leadership; it is not a required visitor route.

**Risks and assumptions:** Aggregation could overstate mastery, flatten developmental evidence, or duplicate material across screens. A3 qualifies Tigo as learning-based evidence and limits Avianca claims to project-definition experience; these qualifications must remain visible in any derived public claim. AS-03 remains relevant if source material does not support a sufficiently defensible collective account.

**Boundaries and open decisions:** Use only the approved A3 Capability names, mappings, narrative themes/roles, prominence, and evolution arc. Do not add a Capability, change a Project mapping or prominence, turn How I Can Help into a technology-skills catalogue, or flatten the ordered evolution arc into an unqualified success list. The final content allocation across How I Can Help, Leadership, Career Journey, and About remains open within their approved responsibilities.
