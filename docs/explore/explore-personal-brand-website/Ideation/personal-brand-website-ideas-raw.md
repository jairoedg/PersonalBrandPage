## Flexibility Pass — HMW Prompts

These generation prompts elaborate selected Framings 3 and 4; they are not additional framing choices.

1. **Narrow / P1:** How might Home connect approved positioning and profile summary to relevant evidence for experience-fit assessment?
2. **Narrow / P2:** How might Project Story Detail make comparable problems, responses, outcomes, and Jairo's contribution available progressively?
3. **Broad / overall objective:** How might the approved screens collectively support relevance and confidence while remaining scannable and non-linear?
4. **Broad / authoritative reference:** How might public stories communicate credibility through defensible evidence and attribution?
5. **Inverted / overload:** How might we overwhelm visitors with dense or disconnected detail—and, by inversion, keep orientation clear while preserving depth?
6. **Inverted / forced funnel:** How might we force every visitor through one route—and, by inversion, support legitimate direct, shorter, and exploratory paths?
7. **Analogical / evidence brief:** How might a Project Story orient visitors first, then make only useful, publication-eligible supporting detail available?

## Raw Ideas

Ideas are intentionally unranked. Tags organize breadth; they are not formal affinity clusters.

1. **[Home orientation | P1]** Pair approved positioning and a concise profile summary with a clear route into Project Story evidence.
2. **[Story evaluation | P2]** Open a Project Story with business context and outcome, then reveal responsibilities, decisions, and deeper evidence progressively.
3. **[Overall experience | P1/P2]** Present the P0 evidence path alongside P1 supporting entry points, without requiring a fixed sequence.
4. **[Credibility | Content Owner]** Distinguish Jairo's evidenced contribution from broader team or project outcomes in each public story.
5. **[Scannability | P2]** Give each meaningful content section a clear primary claim, with supporting depth available according to that section's and screen's purpose.
6. **[Navigation | P1/P2]** Keep global navigation available so visitors can enter, skip, revisit, or move directly to Contact.
7. **[Evidence brief | Content Owner]** Structure a public story around orientation, Jairo's contribution, outcome, and optional eligible detail—without exposing internal Evidence.
8. **[Home evidence | P1]** Use Home previews to connect approved profile context to public Project Stories without choosing preview stories or ordering them now.
9. **[Story depth | P2]** Make the Project Story Detail experience support deeper comparison of problem, response, decisions, and outcomes.
10. **[Supporting context | P1/P2]** Allow a visitor to enter through an approved supporting screen and continue to Projects or a Project Story.
11. **[Evidence | Content Owner]** Include public evidence/reference cues only where eligible and materially useful to understanding or confidence.
12. **[Projects scanning | P1]** Use a consistent high-level preview pattern to aid scanning while leaving exact summary fields open.
13. **[Contextual routes | P2]** Offer related exploration links where the A3 relationships support them, rather than using runtime recommendation logic.
14. **[Story curation | Content Owner]** Present Project Stories as curated public narratives derived from validated Projects, not as raw source records.
15. **[Home routes | P1]** Let Home continue to Projects, supporting context, or Contact according to visitor need.
16. **[Related context | P2]** Connect a Project Story to relevant approved Capability or Leadership context where supported by canonical relationships.
17. **[Confidence | P1/P2]** Let credible evidence build confidence across the experience without making Contact a mandatory final step.
18. **[Capability context | Content Owner]** Present approved business-problem-focused capabilities with supporting Project Stories, not as a technology-skills catalogue.
19. **[Variable depth | P2]** Let story depth reflect its approved narrative purpose rather than imposing one uniform amount of detail.
20. **[P1 paths | P1/P2]** Allow How I Can Help, Leadership, Career Journey, and About to connect to approved evidence or Contact without adding P3-specific navigation.
21. **[Career evolution | Content Owner]** Preserve the approved Tigo → Avianca → DFA learning arc through related stories and career context, without flattening it into an unqualified success list.

## Synectics/Bisociation Blends

**Core tension:** Help visitors orient themselves in rich professional evidence while preserving credibility, depth, and freedom to choose their path. Ecological networks, musical motifs, and field guides were structural analogies used as generative prompts only; they do not add requirements.

22. **[Evidence network]** Treat public Project Stories as connected nodes, with curated links through approved Capabilities and Leadership Principles; visitors choose their own path, with no mandatory sequence or runtime recommendation.
23. **[Theme and variation]** Let an approved leadership or capability theme recur across related stories, while each story retains its own context, evidence, and attribution; the professional-evolution arc remains qualified as approved.
24. **[Layered field guide]** Give each story an orientation layer, followed by deeper public detail suited to its purpose; the precise disclosure interaction remains open.
