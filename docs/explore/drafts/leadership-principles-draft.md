## The seven principles (from the blueprint)

1. Projects succeed because people succeed.
2. Communication uncertainty is often a greater delivery risk than technical uncertainty.
3. Removing unnecessary friction allows technical teams to perform at their highest level.
4. Technology creates value only when it solves real business problems.
5. Predictability builds trust.
6. Every difficult project leaves a lesson worth documenting.
7. Supporting people also means making timely decisions when additional support is no longer helping the individual, the team or the project.
