# Project Story chapter buttons: summaries

**Draft, not approved.** The four button texts per story for D-035 ("The story in 60 seconds"). Every text is a verbatim excerpt from the story; the site uses these texts. Edit here and Claude updates the site.

Rules (D-035): 01 = first sentence of Business Problem or Business Challenge (else Business Context). 02 = the titles of Leadership Decisions or Leadership Focus, without numbers (else the challenge titles); at most 5, or 4 and "and N more". 03 = the story's Outcome line, with a large figure only where approved. 04 = one sentence from Leadership Reflection or What I Learned.

## DCTX

- **01 The situation** (from Business Problem): Because its existing platform can no longer support this vision, DCTX is driving a digital transformation to modernize and unify its core experience.
- **02 What I did** (from Leadership Decisions):
  - Conflict Resolution
  - Pragmatic Adaptation & Late-Stage Crisis Triage
  - Non-Negotiable Deadlines & Recovery Plan
  - Keep Decision-Makers Closely Aligned
- **03 The outcome**: Full inception scope deployed on September 18, after the original July 30 target; the mobile app launched two months late with minimal production bugs.
- **04 What I learned**: It reinforced a fundamental truth: the true value of Agile frameworks lies not in dogmatically following ceremonies, but in utilizing transparency, empirical data, and proactive risk management to enable swift decision-making and continuous delivery flow.

## DFA Capital Markets

- **01 The situation** (from Business Problem): DFA did not have sufficient internal engineering capacity and technical expertise to complete the required integration and refactoring work at the required pace.
- **02 What I did** (from Leadership Decisions):
  - Treat Risks as Triggers for Delivery Improvement
  - Bring in the Right Expertise
  - Escalate Missing Business Support
  - Keep Decision-Makers Closely Aligned
  - Work Collaboratively With the Client
- **03 The outcome**: **5.0 → 8.7** Customer satisfaction · API Gateway delivered, business processes refactored, production go-live supported.
- **04 What I learned**: I learned that the value of Scrum or Kanban does not come from following ceremonies mechanically.

## Avianca

- **01 The situation** (from Business Problem): The existing Oracle Exadata-based analytics solution constrained Avianca's ability to respond quickly to new information demands.
- **02 What I did** (from Leadership Decisions):
  - Reuse Lessons From the Previous Big Data Engagement
  - Create a Table of Experts
  - Escalate the Need for a Data Architect
  - Base Decisions on Available Expertise
- **03 The outcome**: Business case and TCO completed for sponsor approval. See note
- **04 What I learned**: This project reinforced the importance of delegating responsibilities to the people with the right expertise and accountability.

## Tigo

- **01 The situation** (from Business Context): Tigo initiated a strategic program to strengthen SOX compliance controls.
- **02 What I did** (from Major Delivery Problems (no Leadership Decisions)):
  - Insufficient Big Data Expertise
  - Incomplete Understanding of Source Data
  - Architecture Was Not Standardized
  - Weak Product Definition
  - and 3 more
- **03 The outcome**: Not achieved: the planned SOX control monitoring through Tableau dashboards was not delivered, and the original approach was stopped after approximately six months and restructured.

## GT&T Guyana

- **01 The situation** (from Business Context): Greenfield implementation of a new Value Added Services (VAS) platform for the Guyanese telecommunications operator.
- **02 What I did** (from Leadership Focus list (no titled decisions or challenges)):
  - Coordinated implementation teams.
  - Managed technical dependencies.
  - Produced weekly status reports.
  - Managed risks and blockers.
  - Maintained customer communication throughout the project.
- **03 The outcome**: Production go-live completed.
- **04 What I learned**: This project reinforced that disciplined execution, consistent communication, and proactive dependency management are often enough to deliver successful outcomes when the project environment is stable.
